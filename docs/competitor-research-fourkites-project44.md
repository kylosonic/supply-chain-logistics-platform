## FOURKITES

**Hero claim (live):** "AI agents that autonomously execute supply chain operations" — Intelligent Control Tower®, 1.1M carriers/suppliers, 200+ territories, "85% of exceptions resolve themselves," "Digital Workers" (named agents Tracy, Sam, Alan, Polly, Cassie), Loft agent platform (Feb 2026).

- **Positioning:** control tower that "stops problems before they start"; AI centerpiece (agentic + analytical + conversational + visual AI); four problem families: Inbound, Outbound, Yard & Schedule.
- **Product surface:** OTR (FTL/LTL), ocean (Haven), air (Dynamic ETA), rail/intermodal, last mile, parcel; yard (YardWorks AI, AutoGate AI, AutoBooker); Digital Twins; free CarrierLink driver app; Premier Carrier List.
- **Pricing:** NO public pricing (pricing page 404). TrustRadius: "no pricing plans… no free version or trial." Custom enterprise contracts only. No SMB tier.
- **Target segments:** enterprise shippers (Fortune 2000/500: Bayer, Coca-Cola, Walmart, Conagra, Martin Brower). Offices: Chicago, Amsterdam, Munich, Chennai, Singapore — **no Africa office, no Ethiopia presence**.
- **Scale/proof (self-published, internally inconsistent):** 1.1M carriers / 640k carriers (different pages), 3.2M vs 3M daily shipments, 1,600+ customers, 85% exceptions self-resolved, 2.5x capacity (Atkore), 4% OTD (Martin Brower), 19% detention reduction.
- **Recent:** Loft launch (Feb 2026), Agentic Ocean Booking (May 2026); withdrew from Gartner RTTVP MQ (Feb 2025) to control its AI narrative; last corroborated funding Aug 2022 (~$30M, FedEx); a claimed "$225M Series E @ $1.7B (May 2024)" is NOT corroborated — treat as unverified. 2022 layoffs.
- **Integration:** 1,600+ pre-connected businesses; tracking from "any document format — no tech requirements"; ELD/GPS/telematics; EDI/API; SAP/Oracle/Manhattan/BlueYonder ecosystem.
- **Weaknesses:** opaque enterprise-only pricing; no trial; all proof = Fortune 500 cases; thin independent reviews (3 TrustRadius, 8.3/10); marketing-stat drift; funding silence since 2022.

- **Pricing:** NO public pricing (pricing page 404). TrustRadius: "no pricing plans… no free version or trial." Custom enterprise contracts only.

- **Scale/proof (self-published):** 3.7T data points/yr, 700M events/day, 8B external risk signals/day, 282K carriers, 1M+ facilities, 5,000+ API + 1,400+ telematics integrations; ROI cases: $16M cost avoided (Baltimore bridge), $12M/yr safety stock, 99% D&D fee reduction, 86% gate-wait reduction, 72% faster exception response.

## Synthesis — implications for FreightPilot

### Table stakes (hygiene, not differentiators)
1. **Real-time tracking + predictive ETA** — the category pitch is "see disruptions weeks before." FreightPilot must deliver reliable ETA + exception alerts on ETB-denominated routes.
2. **Exception management as the core workflow** — both now sell "exceptions resolve themselves" (85% / 72–85% claims). Same job-to-be-done for FreightPilot: fewer phone calls, faster answers — stated honestly, no invented automation rates.
3. **Low-friction data ingestion** — FourKites: "tracking from any document format — no tech requirements." This validates FreightPilot's sample-data onboarding wedge.
4. **Proof signals** — buyers will ask for proof. FreightPilot's honesty rules forbid fabricating it, so the lesson is: collect and publish REAL local case data early (ETB saved, hours saved per operator).

### What NOT to copy
- **Unverifiable marketing stats** (FourKites' 640k vs 1.1M carrier drift; project44's 3.7T/700M/8B data blizzard). FreightPilot's honesty rules are a positioning asset here.
- **Opaque enterprise-only pricing** ("talk to sales", no public price, no trial) — the single biggest opening for FreightPilot.
- **Analyst-category theater** (FourKites withdrew from Gartner's MQ; don't chase quadrants).
- **Agent mythology without local grounding** (named "digital workers" presuppose mature data pipelines).
- **Massive integration surface** — a small Ethiopian operator needs phone/WhatsApp + bank transfer, not EDI. Build the ~10 integrations that matter locally (CBE, telebirr, common workflows).

### Where FreightPilot differentiates (evidence-backed gaps in the giants)
- **Geography:** neither has an Africa office or Ethiopia presence. FreightPilot owns the local network effect: Ethiopian carriers, Djibouti corridor, ETB economics.
- **Pricing & payments:** neither has a Free tier, trial, or published prices. Free tier + ETB + bank transfer is a category they vacated.
- **Cash-flow reality:** their ROI cases are $16M/$12M enterprise savings; FreightPilot's partial payments + quarterly billing match Ethiopian logistics cash flow.
- **Instant value:** their time-to-value is an enterprise onboarding project; sample-data onboarding is the SMB wedge.
- **Trust:** their 2023–2024 turbulence (layoffs, funding silence) is a chance to be the stable, local, transparent alternative.

### Pricing sanity check
FreightPilot ladder: Free / 4,500 ETB·mo⁻¹ (~$35/mo) / 10,000 ETB·qtr⁻¹ (~$78/qtr) / 45,000 ETB·qtr⁻¹ (~$350/qtr).
- Both incumbents are 100% custom-enterprise with no published floor — **any published ETB price is instantly the most transparent price in the category.** Defensible on transparency alone.
- ~$35/mo ≈ a fraction of one truck-day in Addis — arithmetic works if Starter demonstrably saves ≥1 exception/month.
- Watch the Scale-vs-Starter rate inversion (Scale ≈ $26/mo effective vs Starter $35/mo): communicate Scale as prepayment value, not a price cut.
- The Scale→Enterprise step (4.5×) is the widest — justify with hard deliverables or mid-market buyers churn there.
- **Benchmark against local substitutes (spreadsheets + WhatsApp + telebirr), not FourKites/project44** — that's the real competitive set for Free→Starter conversion.
